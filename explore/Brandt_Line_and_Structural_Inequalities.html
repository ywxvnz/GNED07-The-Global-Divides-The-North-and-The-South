Brandt Line
and Structural
Inequalities

TNCs from developing countries have become increasingly important in the 21st century. The top TNCs in 2007 came from developing countries such as China, India, Malaysia, Singapore, Korea, Philippines, Thailand, Brazil, Mexico, South Africa, and Turkey. These developing countries became major players in industries like cars, electronics, steel, and container shipping.

These evolving conditions suggest that the conception of global South and global North should not be limited to conventional characterization but could be a representation of an emerging form.

The Brandt Line shows that richer countries are mostly located in the Northern Hemisphere, while poorer countries are mostly located in tropical regions and in the Southern Hemisphere.
