The North-South Divide

The North-South divide is based on the degree of economic and political power countries possess. The global North includes rich, industrialized, democratic, and capitalist nations such as the USA, Canada, Western Europe, developed parts of Asia, Australia, and New Zealand.

Another way of classifying states is through human development, which measures people's ability to develop their full potential and lead fulfilled lives. Human development indicators include life expectancy, health, education, literacy, sanitation, food, jobs, and political participation.
